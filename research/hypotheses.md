# Research hypotheses

- H1: Equal linear coefficients with fixed total shortage may yield multiple optimal distributions.
- H2: Equal positive quadratic weights favor balanced absolute shortages under symmetric conditions.
- H3: Convex piecewise linear penalties approximate increasing marginal quadratic penalties depending on breakpoint design.
- H4: Relative MILP and MIQP computational performance depends on formulation and instance structure.
- H5: Business priorities, penalty calibration and production deadlines influence the preferred formulation.

These are hypotheses, not measured results.
