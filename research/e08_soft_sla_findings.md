Analytical oracle: fixed allocation 800, each demand 200, weights [5,4,3,2,1], SLA target 180 each. Initially all shortage 200 is in Central, creating 180 SLA shortfall. Transferring 20 allocated units each from West, East, South, North to Central reduces SLA shortfall by 20 at marginal weighted demand penalty increases 50,100,150,200 respectively. Consequently, for nonnegative mu, optimal aggregate SLA shortfall is 180 below 50, 160 between 50 and 100, 140 between 100 and 150, 120 between 150 and 200, and 100 above 200. At exact thresholds all integer shortfalls between adjacent levels can be optimal. Strict mu>200 guarantees the minimum aggregate SLA shortfall in this instance; it does not guarantee equitable individual SLA service.

Only mu=500 solver output has been supplied. Other coefficients are analytical predictions awaiting validation. Raw objective values across different mu are not comparable as business savings.
